# Flat dial refinement 08

2026-10-23. User requested simplification of concept 07 and small-size inspection. Generated using built-in image_gen with concept 07 as edit reference; PNG remains a concept, not an installed icon.

[Master PNG](icon-concept-08-flat-dial.png)

[Size comparison](size-check-08/comparison.png)

Mechanical size exports and comparison composed with ImageMagick, without changing the design: 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 pixels, on #171b22 and #f1f3f5. Source alpha retained in individual PNGs.

Visual findings: broad arc and pointer remain recognizable at 32–64px; 24px is usable but simplified. At 16px on dark background the charcoal body loses separation and cyan resembles a power-button mark. A tray-specific variant is still needed. Generated raster also retains slight shading and edge irregularities; exact flat vector geometry is a future production step. No native desktop installation test was performed.

Prompt:

Refine the provided rotary-control app icon, keeping its identity and composition: circular charcoal dial, cyan quarter arc at upper right and cyan upright indicator at 12 o'clock. Make it a STRICTLY FLAT minimalist vector-style icon with solid fills only. Remove ALL bevels, metallic rims, thin outlines, gradients, gloss, highlights, shadows, depth, texture and 3D. Outer thick ring solid graphite #343b46, inner circular knob solid charcoal #222831, broad transparent circular gap between ring and knob. Electric cyan #00d5ec quarter arc is a thick clean continuous 90-degree band from 12 to 3 o'clock with modest rounded ends. Inner indicator is a bold rounded cyan vertical bar thick enough to survive at 16px, from upper area toward center. Only these four main shapes. Perfect circular silhouette, exact frontal orthographic view. No text, no letters, no ticks, no square tile. Transparent background and transparent gap. Occupy 90% of square canvas with equal margins. Designed for recognizability at 16, 24, 32 pixels; broad forms and clean edges.
